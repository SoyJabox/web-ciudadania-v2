## Brand & Style
The design system embodies an enlightened, academic, yet warmly approachable pedagogical presence for digital citizenship and knowledge society studies. It rejects cold algorithmic sterility in favor of a timeless, tactile editorial aesthetic—bridging classic humanistic print scholarship with modern open-web performance. 

The aesthetic is characterized as **Warm Editorial / Modern Academic**:
- Generous, breathable layouts reminiscent of high-end independent journals and literary publications.
- High typographic tension between an expressive, literary serif headline treatment and clean, utilitarian geometric sans-serif microcopy.
- Earthy, deliberate materiality: warm paper grounds, deep vegetable-based ink contrast, pill badges, and delicate rules framing educational modules with poise and clarity.

## Colors
The palette evokes archival botanical prints, unbleached cotton paper, and pressed botanical pigments. High legibility and quiet authority govern every pairing.

### Core Roles
- **Canvas / Background**: `#fbfaf7` (Warm Paper). Replaces sterile white with an organic, low-fatigue substrate.
- **Ink / Typography**: `#243d35` (Deep Forest Ink). Serves as the primary foreground color, delivering rigorous AA/AAA contrast without the harshness of pitch black.
- **Primary Action (CTA)**: `#284e3f` (Rich Forest Green). Paired with `#fbfaf7` for sharp, decisive primary interactive triggers.
- **Structural Chrome / Header**: `#e9eee2` (Soft Sage Wash). Forms navigation banners, utility rails, and subtle foundational separations.
- **Card Surface**: `#ffffff` with a `#d8dfd7` fine structural stroke.

### Editorial Signature Accents
- **Sage Accent**: `#91ad96` (Annotations, active states, module metadata).
- **Ochre Accent**: `#d6b674` (Highlights, callout bars, historical timelines).
- **Terracotta Accent**: `#c89980` (Discussion prompts, critical thinking triggers, key concepts).
- **Slate Blue Accent**: `#8fa9b5` (Digital tools, documentation, references).

### Thematic Unit Washes
Used exclusively for section hero mastheads, thematic unit banners, and callout container surfaces:
- **Unit 1 (Sage)**: `#dce5d9`
- **Unit 2 (Warm Ochre)**: `#e8deca`
- **Unit 3 (Soft Terracotta)**: `#e9dad2`
- **Unit 4 (Soft Slate Blue)**: `#dbe3e8`

## Typography
Typography is the spine of the experience. It establishes academic rigor through **Newsreader** (featuring delicate serifs, bookish proportions, and fluid italics) while preserving pristine legibility in functional UI contexts with **DM Sans**.

- **Editorial Serifs (`Newsreader`)**: Reserved for module headings, lesson titles, chapter introductions, and pull quotes. Italics should be used intentionally for emphasis, pedagogical inquiries, and foreign terms.
- **Contemporary Sans (`DM Sans`)**: Applied to long-form reading bodies, syllabus lists, metadata pills, navigation, and interactive inputs. The geometric purity balances the warmth of the serif.
- **Optical Ergonomics**: Long-form body line height is set deliberately wide (1.65–1.85) with an optimal line length capped between 62–75 characters (`max-w-prose`) to avoid eye fatigue.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a disciplined, asymmetrical editorial grid inspired by modern static-site architectures and scholarly periodicals.

- **Desktop (1024px+)**: A 12-column asymmetrical grid with `2.5rem` gutters and a maximum layout bound of `1280px`. Typical page structures feature a 3-column sticky index/table of contents paired with an 8-column main study reading track (leaving 1 column for breathable margin).
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px)**: An 8-column fluid grid, collapsing utility rails beneath content headers with `1.5rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: A single-column vertical stack with `1.25rem` outer margins.
- **Rhythm**: Generous vertical section spacing (`3rem` to `5rem`) provides an unhurried, contemplative reading tempo across lesson modules.

## Elevation & Depth
In alignment with printed matter and tactile paper craft, this design system avoids heavy drop shadows, synthetic blurs, or plastic skeuomorphism. Depth is achieved via **tonal stacking, subtle border rules, and micro-relief**:

- **Paper Layering**: Ground surfaces sit at `#fbfaf7`. Elevated elements (cards, reading sheets) are cast in pure `#ffffff`.
- **Low-Contrast Framing**: Structural elements are delineated with crisp 1px hairpins (`#d8dfd7`), giving the impression of finely trimmed deckle edges.
- **Micro-Shadow (Interactive Only)**: Elevated cards or floating toolbars feature a barely perceptible, diffuse shadow tinted with the primary ink:
  `box-shadow: 0 2px 8px -2px rgba(36, 61, 53, 0.06), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(36, 61, 53, 0.04);`
- **Active / Pressed State**: Eliminates elevation entirely, shifting downward 1px to simulate physical paper compression.

## Shapes
Geometry balances structured scholarship with gentle human touchpoints:
- **Structural Containers & Cards**: Modest, softened corners (`0.5rem` / `8px`) maintain structural stability and respect the grid.
- **Large Content Sections / Hero Banners**: Framed with `1rem` or `1.5rem` corners to soften transition between unit themes.
- **Badges, Tags, & Buttons**: Fully pill-shaped (`9999px`) to create an inviting, human counterweight against the linear academic grid.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid `#284e3f` background, `#fbfaf7` typography, pill shape (`rounded-full`), padded with `space-sm` vertical and `space-lg` horizontal. Subtle scale transition (98%) on click.
- **Secondary / Ghost**: Transparent fill, 1px border in `#243d35`, `#243d35` label. On hover, fills with `#e9eee2`.
- **Pill Badges & Meta Chips**: Height `28px`, pill-shaped, using 10% tinted backgrounds of the respective signature accent (Sage, Ochre, Terracotta, Slate Blue) with high-contrast foreground text and tracking set to uppercase `0.05em`.

### Cards & Content Modules
- **Reading / Activity Cards**: Pure white (`#ffffff`) background, wrapped in a 1px border of `#d8dfd7`, `0.5rem` border radius, padded with `1.75rem`. 
- **Unit Lesson Header Card**: Coated in the respective pastel tone (e.g., `#dce5d9` for Unit 1), featuring an expressive `Newsreader` headline, breadcrumb metadata, and an editorial summary framed by a 1px border tinted 15% darker than the background.

### Form Inputs & Text Fields
- **Fields**: Background `#ffffff`, border 1px `#d8dfd7`, radius `0.5rem`. Focus ring uses `#284e3f` with a 2px offset on `#fbfaf7`.
- **Labels**: Set in `DM Sans` (`label-md`), deep green ink, positioned cleanly above inputs with `0.35rem` spacing.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Controls**: 18px rounded squares (checkbox) or circles (radio), `#ffffff` background with 1.5px `#284e3f` border. When selected, fills with `#284e3f` bearing an ink-cream checkmark icon.

### Editorial Additions
- **Socratic Callout Box**: A left-bordered block (4px solid `#d6b674` or `#c89980`) set upon a 30% wash of the respective unit tone, featuring italicized serif inquiry text.
- **Footnote / Glossary Drawer**: Clean slide-over pane framed by hairpins with `#243d35` typography, maintaining context without navigating away from the core curriculum text.
